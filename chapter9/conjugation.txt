寄せ箸（よせばし）,pulling with chopsticks,器のなかに箸をかけて、自分のほうに寄せること。,Using your chopsticks to hook onto and pull it towards yourself.
重ね箸（かさねばし）,repeated eating,ひとつの料理ばかりを続けて食べる事。,Continuing to eat only one dish instead of alternating between different dishes.
ねぶり箸（ねぶりばし）,licking chopsticks,箸の先をなめたり口にくわえたりすること。,Licking or sucking on the ends of your chopsticks.
移り箸（うつりばし）,switching dishes,箸をつけたのに別の料理を移ること。,Touching one dish with your chopsticks but then moving to another dish.
刺し箸（さしばし）,stabbing food,食べ物に箸を突き刺して口に入れること。,Stabbing food with your chopsticks and putting it into your mouth.
もぎ箸（もぎばし）,removing food with your mouth,箸についた汚れを、口でもぎ取ること。,Using your mouth to remove food or residue stuck on your chopsticks.
振り上げ箸（ふりあげばし）,waving or pointing chopsticks,箸で物や人を指したり振り上げたりすること。,Pointing at people or objects with chopsticks or waving and raising them around.
渡し箸（わたしばし）,resting chopsticks across a bowl,箸を休ませるとき茶碗などに箸をかけ渡すこと。,When taking a break from eating laying your chopsticks across the top of a bowl.
なみだ箸（なみだばし）,dripping chopsticks,料理の汁をポタポタ落としながら食べること。,Eating while letting liquid or sauce drop from your food or chopsticks.
せせり箸（せせりばし）,using chopsticks like a toothpick,箸で歯をつつきようじがわりに使うこと。,Using chopsticks like a toothpick to pick your teeth.
ご飯に箸を立てる（ごはんにはしをたてる）,sticking chopsticks upright in rice,仏前のお供えと同じ形のためタブー。,Sticking chopsticks vertically into rice is taboo because it resembles rice offered to the dead at a Buddhist altar.
押し込み箸（おしこみばし）,pushing food into your mouth,口に入れたものをさらに箸で押し込むこと。,Using chopsticks to push food farther into your mouth after you have already put it in.
迷い箸（まよいばし）,hovering or hesitating chopsticks,箸を皿から皿へと迷わすこと。,Moving your chopsticks back and forth over different dishes because you cannot decide what to take.
探り箸（さぐりばし）,searching through food with chopsticks,料理を箸で探ってみること。,Using your chopsticks to search or probe through food.
拾い箸（ひろいばし）,two people grabbing the same food,２人が同じ料理を箸でつまむこと。,Two people picking up the same piece of food with their chopsticks at the same time.
かき込み箸（かきこみばし）,shoveling food,器に直接口をつけて、箸でかき込んで食べること。,Putting your mouth directly against the bowl and using your chopsticks to shovel food into your mouth.
