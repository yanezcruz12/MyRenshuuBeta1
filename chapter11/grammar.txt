当たり(あたり),Per or for each,このアルバイトでは、一時間当たり千五百円もらえます。,At this part-time job I earn fifteen hundred yen per hour.
～さ,Turns an adjective into a noun,このかばんの重さを調べてください。,Please check the weight of this bag.
～をはじめ,Including a main example,大阪をはじめ、日本のいろいろな都市を訪れたいです。,I want to visit various cities in Japan including Osaka as a main example.
～さえ～ば,As long as or if only,時間さえあれば、毎日日本語を練習できます。,As long as I have time I can practice Japanese every day.
～をもとに,Based on,日本での経験をもとに、作文を書きました。,I wrote an essay based on my experiences in Japan.
